Features & Capabilities

The Six Pillars of Report-IQ

A purpose-built client-side analytical platform combining deterministic precision, mathematical safety, and universal interoperability.

Pillar 1: Privacy First

Zero-Trust Client-Side Privacy

Raw spreadsheets never leave your device. File parsing, data cleaning, relational joins, and statistical regressions execute purely in browser memory.

  • Zero Bytes Uploaded: Core spreadsheet processing runs in your browser.
  • Bank-Grade Confidentiality: Customer PII and trade secrets stay strictly in RAM.
  • Offline-Ready Execution: Load datasets, filter, and report without any internet connection.
  • Metadata-Only AI: Mathematical models verified locally; only schema metadata queried.
Pillar 2: Statistical AI

AI Dataset Insights & Dashboards

Instantly discover statistical relationships, correlations, and co-dependencies across complex multi-column spreadsheets with mathematical certainty.

  • Automated Executive Decks: Presentation-ready KPI cards, business highlights, and risk alerts.
  • IQR Outlier Detection: Computes Q1, Q3, and IQR boundaries to plot distribution anomaly histograms.
  • Pearson Correlation Matrix: Multi-variable linear coefficients identify revenue and risk drivers.
Pillar 3: Audit Engine

AI Report & Sheet Validator

Audit dual-sheet ledgers against bank statements, billing sheets, or partner feeds in one click with a built-in recursive-descent formula parser.

  • Dual-Sheet Reconciliation: Match counterparty entries and flag mismatched totals in seconds.
  • Pratt Formula AST: Parses IF, ISBLANK, COALESCE, ROUND, ABS, MIN, and MAX with financial precision.
  • Multi-Format Extractor: Ingest tabular data from PDFs, Word (.docx), CSVs, and text files.
  • Interactive Clarification: Targeted dialog resolves ambiguous mappings or tolerances.
Pillar 4: Data Prep Studio

Complete Data Preparation Studio

Execute visual SQL-grade relational database joins, smart dataset partitions, ML train/test splits, and intelligent null imputation without writing code.

  • Visual Merge Wizard: Inner, Left Outer, Right Outer, Full Outer joins, and multi-file union appends.
  • Smart Split Wizard: Partition by chunk thresholds, ML splits (80/20, 70/30), or categorical values.
  • Quality & Imputation: Profile missing cells; fill nulls with Mean, Median, Mode, or custom fallbacks.
  • Compound Query Builder: Nested boolean AND/OR conjunctions, regex, and numerical filters.
Pillar 5: Universal Export

Universal 27-Format Export Hub

Compile cleaned and reconciled datasets into 27 industry-standard formats directly in browser RAM without server-side translation overhead.

  • Spreadsheets: Modern Excel (.xlsx), Binary Excel (.xlsb), Legacy (.xls), and OpenDocument (.ods).
  • Big Data & Binary DBs: SQLite 3 (.sqlite), Apache Parquet (.parquet), and Arrow / Feather (.arrow).
  • Executive Documents: AutoTable PDF decks, Word (.docx) XML tables, and standalone HTML reports.
  • Dialect SQL: Production DDL/INSERT scripts for PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, Snowflake, and SQL Server.
Pillar 6: High Performance

High-Performance Workspace

Built for frictionless everyday data manipulation with a 60fps virtualized data grid and deep column health profiling tools.

  • Virtualized Grid: Scroll tens of thousands of rows smoothly with frozen headers and pinned columns.
  • Recharts Visualizer: Dynamic Bar, Line, Pie, Donut, Area, and Scatter charts with theme switching.
  • Column Health Profiler: Data type inference, distinct cardinality counts, and null percentages.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about our local-first security architecture and capabilities.

Do my spreadsheets get sent to your servers?

No, absolutely not. Report-IQ is built from the ground up on a local-first, zero-cloud data egress architecture. File parsing, formula evaluation, relational joins, outlier calculations, and big data compilations all execute purely inside your browser's RAM and Web Workers.

How large of a file can Report-IQ handle?

Report-IQ easily handles multi-tab spreadsheets and datasets containing hundreds of thousands of rows. Our virtualized grid and streaming Web Worker parser maintain a smooth 60fps scrolling experience without freezing your browser interface.

How does Report-IQ prevent AI hallucinations?

Unlike generic chatbots that guess numbers, Report-IQ binds every AI-generated summary, KPI figure, and anomaly flag to client-side verified numbers using RFC 6901 JSON Pointers (`dataRef`). If a number isn't mathematically proven in your dataset, the AI cannot output it.

What formats can I import and export?

You can import modern Excel (.xlsx, .xlsb, .xls), CSV, TSV, JSON, PDF tables, and Word documents. You can export into 27 industry-standard formats, including Apache Parquet, SQLite 3 Binary DBs, Apache Arrow, Word (.docx), AutoTable PDF, multi-dialect SQL, and LaTeX.

Can I use Report-IQ completely offline?

Yes. Once the workspace application is loaded in your browser cache, you can disconnect from the internet entirely. All data preparation, SQL joins, filtering, and 27-format compilation tasks run 100% air-gapped on your local hardware.
